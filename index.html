Clothing Arbitrage AI

Personal clothing resale research and inventory analysis project.

About the project

Clothing Arbitrage AI is a personal research tool being developed to help identify and evaluate secondhand, vintage and designer clothing available through online marketplaces.

How it works

The application is intended to use authorised marketplace APIs to research publicly available listings, evaluate potential resale opportunities and calculate estimated resale profitability. Estimates are subject to human review and independently obtained comparable-sales evidence.

Purpose

The tool is for personal use only and is not offered as a software service. Purchasing decisions, product authentication and resale activities are performed manually. Items identified may be purchased for subsequent resale.

Data use

The application is intended to access marketplace data only through authorised methods and in accordance with applicable API terms. It does not require access to other users' private accounts.
